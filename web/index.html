How it works

A dispute, settled by an AI jury — autonomously

No human touches the settlement loop. The contract convenes the panel, the panel rules, and anyone can finalize.

1

Open a case

A consumer contract calls openCase(evidence) with the dispute and the panel fee.

2

AI panel runs

5 Somnia validators each run the same LLM inference, constrained to PAYEE / PAYER / SPLIT.

3

Majority verdict

Consensus on a byte-identical result. Each juror's reasoning is kept in an on-chain receipt.

4

Stake to appeal

The loser can post a stake and force a re-trial by a larger 9-agent panel with new evidence.

5

Finalize

After the appeal window, anyone calls finalize — funds settle, stake slashed if upheld.

Built as a primitive

One court. Any protocol or agent can call it.

FAQ

Questions

What is Verdikt? ⌄

An on-chain arbitration court. When a deal is contested, the contract convenes a panel of validator-run LLM agents that review the evidence and return a binding verdict — PAYEE, PAYER, or SPLIT — under majority consensus. It's a reusable settlement primitive any contract or agent can call.

How is the AI judgment trust-minimized? ⌄

A subcommittee of Somnia validators each runs the same LLM inference, constrained to a fixed set of allowed outputs, and consensus is reached on a byte-identical result. No single off-chain bot decides — the verdict is the agreed output of the validator panel, with each juror's reasoning kept in an on-chain receipt.

How is it different from Kleros? ⌄

Kleros and similar systems use human jurors staking tokens — slow and uneconomical for small or fast disputes. Verdikt replaces the human jury with an AI panel reaching validator consensus in seconds, while keeping the economic security: a stake-backed appeal escalates to a larger 9-agent panel and slashes frivolous challenges.

Is it actually live? ⌄

Yes. The full stack is deployed on Somnia Shannon testnet. The determinism gate passed (a 5-agent panel returned a byte-identical verdict), real escrow disputes have been settled by live AI panels with no human in the loop, and four final rulings are recorded in the on-chain precedent index. Try the live demo.

What can build on it? ⌄

Anything with a subjective dispute: escrow, parametric insurance, DAO grant clawbacks, freelance milestones, agent-to-agent deals, prediction-market resolution. Six consumers already settle on one shared court — the court doesn't know what any of them mean, which is the point.

Is it audited? ⌄

It's unaudited hackathon code on testnet. There is an internal security review (SECURITY.md) and a 141-test suite including fuzz and invariant tests. A third-party audit is a prerequisite for mainnet.
